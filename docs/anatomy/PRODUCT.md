# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: single standalone HTML file (anatomy kit + vanilla JS + inlined three.js). Assumed because the brief asked for "html" and nobody was available to answer.

## Users
ASSUMED (no one could answer the interview): Korean middle-school students (13–15) learning the digestive system in science class, alone or projected by a teacher. Bilingual: Korean is the learner's language, English is the reference vocabulary.

## Product Purpose
An interactive anatomy model of the human torso. A learner can lift each organ out of the body, enlarge it, turn it in 3D, and read its name in English and Korean. The body starts closed (intact skin); a scalpel makes a long midline cut, the incision is drawn open into a wide window, and the camera looks down from a surgeon's point of view onto the organs. A guided "follow the meal" mode shows how food is digested from mouth to anus and which organ does what. Success: the learner can name each organ in both languages, place it in the body, and say what it does to a bite of food.

## Positioning
A physical-feeling dissection model, not a textbook diagram: organs come out of the body and go back in, and the digestion route is played on the model itself.

## Operating Context
Used on a laptop, a classroom projector or a phone. Single offline HTML file; no network at runtime.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Organs: heart (심장), lungs (폐), liver (간), gallbladder (쓸개/담낭), stomach (위), pancreas (췌장), spleen (비장), small intestine (소장), large intestine (대장), esophagus (식도), mouth/teeth (입), plus rectum (직장). The brief wrote "취장"; read as 췌장 (pancreas). Mouth is shown as a stop on the food route.
- Organs are removable from the body, enlargeable and rotatable.
- English and Korean names for every organ.
- Educational content is simplified to middle-school level and is not medical advice.
- Organ shapes are procedurally modelled and stylised, not scans.

## Evidence on Hand
No supplied imagery or data. All shapes, text and timings are authored here and are illustrative.

## Product Principles
- The model is the explanation: everything is learned by touching an organ.
- Every label is bilingual, always.
- Facts shown are simplified but true.
- Nothing needs a manual: the first interaction is obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard operable organ list, readable Korean type, reduced-motion respected, contrast ≥4.5:1.
